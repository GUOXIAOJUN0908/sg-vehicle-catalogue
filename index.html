CATEGORY GUIDE

Understanding COE categories

Which vehicles belong to each Certificate of Entitlement category in Singapore.

CAT A

Smaller & family cars

Applies to: Non-fully electric cars with engines up to 1,600cc and maximum power output up to 97kW (130bhp); and fully electric cars with maximum power output up to 110kW (147bhp).

Typical use: Everyday family sedans and compact commuter cars.

CAT B

Larger & luxury cars

Applies to: Non-fully electric cars with engines above 1,600cc or maximum power output above 97kW (130bhp); and fully electric cars above 110kW (147bhp).

Typical use: Larger, more powerful, luxury, and high-performance electric cars.

CAT C

Goods vehicles & buses

Applies to: Commercial vehicles including lorries, vans, heavy trucks, and buses.

Typical use: Vehicles operated by businesses and commercial transport providers.

CAT D

Motorcycles

Applies to: Motorcycles and other two-wheeled motor vehicles.

Typical use: Personal and commercial two-wheel transport; premiums are generally the lowest among the five categories.

CAT E

Open category

Applies to: Any vehicle type except motorcycles in Category D.

Typical use: Commonly used for larger luxury cars or as an alternative when Category A or B supply is tight.

Definitions follow current LTA rules. Further reading: LTA OneMotoring, Wise, and the Singapore Mandarin Database.
